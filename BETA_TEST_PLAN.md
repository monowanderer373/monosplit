# TabbyTally Beta Test Plan

## Goal

Validate whether new users can use TabbyTally during a real group trip without your help.

## Test Setup

- Use iPhone Safari.
- Open the live URL.
- Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- Start from the home-screen icon during the trip.
- Ask testers to avoid asking you for help unless they are fully blocked.

## Core Tasks

1. Create or join a trip.
2. Choose who they are in the trip.
3. Add at least three expenses:
   - one simple equal split
   - one expense where not everyone joined
   - one expense with a different currency if possible
4. Open Dashboard and explain what they think they owe or are owed.
5. Open Settle Up and explain who should pay whom.
6. Copy personal statement and group statement.

## Feedback Questions

1. What were you trying to do when you felt confused?
2. Was Add Expense clear step by step?
3. Did you understand which split method to use?
4. Did Dashboard show the information you cared about first?
5. Did Settle Up clearly explain who should pay whom?
6. Did the included items help you trust the numbers?
7. Would you share the statement in a real friend group?
8. What made the app feel unfinished?
9. What would you pay for, if anything?

## Success Criteria

- A new user can add an expense without explanation.
- A new user can identify their own payment responsibility in under 30 seconds.
- A group can agree on the final settlement without reading raw tables.
- Testers can describe what the app does in one sentence.
