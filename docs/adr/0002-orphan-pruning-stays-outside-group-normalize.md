# Orphan-reference pruning stays outside `normalizeGroup`

`src/lib/groupNormalize.ts` (`normalizeExpense`, `normalizeSettlementPayment`, `normalizeGroup`) fixes up field-level shape and legacy schema drift (missing `payerIds`, dirty currency codes, stale/negative amounts, missing defaults). It deliberately does **not** remove expenses, settlement payments, or comments that reference a `personId` no longer in `group.people` — that pruning stays as a separate step, done only inside the Zustand persist `partialize` (outbound, before writing to localStorage/Supabase).

Before this change, `migrateGroupData` (used by `replaceGroup`/`upsertGroup` for inbound sync) never pruned orphans, while `partialize` (outbound persist) always did. It would have been natural to fold both into one `normalizeGroup` and prune everywhere. We rejected that: in multi-device sync, a person and an expense referencing them can arrive out of order, so pruning on the inbound path risks deleting a legitimate expense just because its payer hasn't synced yet. If a future change wants inbound pruning too, it needs to be deliberate and paired with a look at sync ordering guarantees — not an incidental side effect of merging normalizers.
